Welcome!

I am a postdoctoral fellow at the Faculty of Business and Economics of the University of Hong Kong.

I received a Ph.D. in Finance from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology in 2026.

My research focuses on information economics and financial intermediation. I study how information production and disclosure shape the decisions of investors, firms, and financial intermediaries.
